Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Little Steps safety, booking, and care standards.

How does Little Steps vet caregivers and daycare centers?
Every daycare center undergoes an in-person 42-point safety audit covering cleanliness, fire safety, childproofing, and CCTV coverage. All individual caregivers must pass government ID verification, police background verification, and certified pediatric first aid checks.
Can I book childcare on short notice or at night?
Yes! We specialize in 24×7 childcare. Centers with the "24/7 Shift" badge accept emergency drop-ins with as little as 45 minutes notice, accommodating hospital shift workers, corporate emergencies, and late travels.
How does the Live CCTV feed work for parents?
Once your child is checked in, a secure, encrypted video stream link is activated in your Parent Dashboard. You can check in on your child anytime directly from your phone or laptop.
What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule?
Hourly drop-ins can be canceled up to 2 hours before the slot for a 100% instant refund or wallet credit. Weekly and monthly plans can be paused or transferred if you're traveling.
